Sadness Fever

Album · 2024 · CCL-009

The first album released under the Always Your Beauty name captures an incarnation of the project that desperately tried to become a full band, shaped by external pressure to meet the expectations of the local live scene. Hybernesque

EP · 2020 · CCL-006

This EP marks a turning point for Octavio Juarez as a singer, songwriter, and producer. Across these recordings, he confronts his insecurities around recording and production, along with the fear of embracing a solo path. In doing so, he lays the groundwork for what would become a malleable, incarnation-based collective. These songs form the true foundation of the project’s sound.
